How to play

Four teams each manage a different household over six rounds, from 2028 to 2040. Choose affordable investments, explain decisions, then ask the lecturer to advance the round. Each action can be purchased once.

The lecturer advances only after all teams have submitted. Outcomes reflect transparent illustrative teaching rules, not real-world forecasts.

This prototype operates in one browser using local storage. Students on separate devices will not synchronise. A shared backend is required for simultaneous classroom use.

For a 10:00–16:00 workshop, reserve 45 minutes for lunch and 15:40–16:00 for facilitated reflection.
